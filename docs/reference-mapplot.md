# MapPlot

**Map plots are general-purpose maps of the sky.** They support a variety of [projections](reference-projections.md) (including a few perspective based projections that help show what the sky looks like at a particular time and place).

Stars on map plots are plotted in their [_astrometric_ positions](reference-positions.md).
