# Notes on the German translation

German translations of stars, constellation names, and DSO objects
are derived from:

- Paul Ahnert, _Kleine oraktische Astronomie_, Johann Ambrosius Barth 1983
- Sigel, _Sehenswertes am Sternenhimmel_, Verlag MIR Moskau und Fachbuchverlag Leipzig 1986, ISBN 3-343-00178-3 
- Sven Andersson. _Blick in die Sternenwelt 2026_, Förderverein von Archenhold-Sternwarte und Zeiss-Groß-Planetarium e.V., ISBN 3-86021-053-X 

## Constellations

Some constellation names are available in German, while others remain in their original form.

## Stars

There are very few star names that have German equivalents. 

## Deep Sky Objects

Most DSOs have German names.
